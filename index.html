How to create a high-quality QR code

  1. Enter your destination. Add a website link, menu, contact detail, or message to the generator.
  2. Customize your design. Choose a high-contrast color combination and add your logo or business mark.
  3. Download and test it. Download the print-ready PNG and scan it from the size and distance where people will use it.

Good to know / 03

Frequently Asked Questions

Are these QR codes free for commercial use?
Yes. You can use the QR codes you create with MarkQR for restaurants, storefronts, campaigns, and other commercial projects.
Do QR codes created with MarkQR expire?
No. MarkQR creates static QR codes that do not depend on a subscription or redirect service. They continue to work as long as the encoded destination is available.
Is my information uploaded?
No. QR generation and logo processing happen locally in your browser, so your content and uploaded logo stay on your device.
